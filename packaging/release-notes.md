A static recompilation of the arcade game **NBA Showtime: NBA on NBC** for Windows and Linux. No game data is included: you need your own ROM set.

**Your ROM set:** one zip holding MAME's `nbashowt.zip` and `nbashowt/nbashowt.chd`.

**With RecompLauncher:** install NBA Showtime Recomp, pick your ROM zip on the setup screen and press Play.

**On its own:** download the `Windows-x64.zip` or `Linux-x64.tar.gz` file and unpack it anywhere. Run `nbalauncher`, choose your ROM zip, press **Build**, then **Play**.

To update, unpack a new release over the old folder. Your build, settings and saves stay.

See the [README](https://github.com/PlayingKarrde/NBAShowtime-Recomp#readme) for controls and options.
